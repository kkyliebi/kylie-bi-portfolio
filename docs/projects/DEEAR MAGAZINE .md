**Project Overview**
Deear Magazine is an editorial publication project exploring identity, migration, emotional memory, and queer narratives through contemporary publication design systems.
The project investigates how editorial structures can shape emotional pacing, narrative rhythm, and visual storytelling experiences.

**Core Concept**
An editorial publication project exploring identity, migration, emotional narratives, and queer storytelling through contemporary publication design systems.
The project focused on constructing emotionally-driven reading experiences through typography, layout rhythm, and visual narrative consistency.

**Context / Question**
How can editorial design communicate emotional and cultural narratives beyond textual content alone?
The project explores publication design as a narrative system where typography, sequencing, imagery, and spatial rhythm collectively construct meaning.

**Concept Development**
The publication was developed around emotionally-driven editorial storytelling.
Rather than prioritizing decorative layout design, the project focused on creating visual pacing systems capable of supporting fragmented narratives, personal memories, and identity-based storytelling.

**The design system integrated:**
· typography hierarchies
· visual sequencing
· spatial rhythm
· image-text relationships
· publication consistency
