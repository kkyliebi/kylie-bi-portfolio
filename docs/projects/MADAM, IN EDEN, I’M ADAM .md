**Project Overview**
Madam, in Eden, I’m Adam is a conceptual photography project exploring identity, symbolic narrative, body language, and emotional tension through cinematic visual storytelling.
The project investigates how staged imagery and visual symbolism can communicate psychological and interpersonal narratives beyond verbal language.

**Core Concept**
A conceptual visual storytelling project exploring symbolic identity, body language, and cyclical narrative structures through staged photography and visual semiotics.
The project investigates emotional and psychological narratives through carefully constructed visual symbolism and cinematic image sequencing.

**Context / Question**
How can visual narratives communicate emotional complexity through symbolism, gesture, and spatial relationships rather than explicit storytelling?
The project explores photography as a narrative system capable of constructing layered emotional and symbolic meaning.

**Concept Development**
The project was developed around cyclical narrative structures, symbolic imagery, and staged visual composition.

**The visual language focused on:**
· body movement
· environmental symbolism
· emotional atmosphere
· visual repetition
· cinematic pacing
· symbolic spatial relationships
The photographic narrative was approached as a sequence-based storytelling system rather than a collection of isolated images.
