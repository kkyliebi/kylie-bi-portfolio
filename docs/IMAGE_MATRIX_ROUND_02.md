# Image Matrix — Round 02

**Status:** Second-pass selection framework  
**Purpose:** Convert the role-based Round 01 edit into a file-level screening system that is fast, defensible and ready for contact-sheet review.  
**Source:** `docs/IMAGE_MATRIX_ROUND_01.md`  
**Scope:** TYRANNO; The Infinite Discussion; Audi Integrated Brand Communication; Deear Magazine; Madam, in Eden, I'm Adam

## 1. Round 02 objective

Round 01 established what each project needs to prove.

Round 02 now evaluates actual candidate images against four questions:

1. Does the image perform a distinct narrative role?
2. Does it visibly support Kylie's authorship, contribution or decision-making?
3. Is the image publication-safe, historically accurate and technically usable?
4. Does it strengthen the portfolio as one coherent communication archive?

Round 02 is not the final sequence. It is the disciplined reduction before sequencing.

## 2. Decision labels

- **KEEP:** strong enough for the first job-ready release.
- **MAYBE:** potentially useful, but weaker than another candidate or dependent on sequence.
- **HOLD:** blocked by permission, authorship, confidentiality, factual status or missing context.
- **CUT:** redundant, decorative, weak, misleading or technically unsuitable.
- **MISSING:** a required image role still has no viable candidate.

## 3. File-level scoring system

Score each candidate from 0–2 in every category.

| Code | Criterion | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NR | Narrative role | unclear / redundant | partially useful | performs one clear role immediately |
| AU | Authorship evidence | contribution invisible | contribution inferred | contribution or decision-making is legible |
| CL | Communication clarity | confusing without explanation | understandable with caption | understandable at a glance |
| CR | Credibility | generic / staged / misleading | plausible but weakly evidenced | specific, real and historically accurate |
| PQ | Publication quality | unusable | usable with repair | strong enough as-is |
| PS | Publication safety | unresolved | conditionally safe | cleared / owned / properly attributable |
| CO | Coherence | conflicts with archive direction | neutral | strengthens the shared visual system |

**Maximum score:** 14

### Score interpretation

- **12–14:** KEEP
- **9–11:** MAYBE
- **0–8:** CUT unless the image fills a critical missing role
- **Any unresolved PS or authorship issue:** HOLD regardless of total

## 4. Fast elimination rules

Cut before scoring when any of the following is true:

- near-duplicate of a stronger image;
- decorative mood image with no evidentiary role;
- generic mock-up that replaces rather than documents the actual outcome;
- unreadable screenshot;
- process wall with no legible transformation;
- image requires a long paragraph to become relevant;
- misleading project status;
- unclear authorship or publication permission;
- inconsistent colour treatment that cannot be repaired without distortion;
- image quality too low for desktop presentation.

Use one row per candidate image.

## 6. Project-specific Round 02 checks

### 6.1 TYRANNO

**Required proof:** research depth, visual-system construction, motion logic, world-building and interaction.

Prioritise candidates that show:

- behaviour or anatomy translated into glyph logic;
- one readable development sequence;
- classification or symbol-family structure;
- static-to-motion transformation;
- final glyph system;
- truthful website / translator status;
- one material thesis-book image only if strong.

Cut or hold:

- decorative dinosaur imagery;
- repeated specimens;
- unsupported scientific certainty;
- AR presented as completed when it remains proposed;
- visually attractive images that do not explain the communication system.

**Round 02 shortlist target:** 10–13 candidates  
**Final release target:** 8–10 images

### 6.2 The Infinite Discussion

**Required proof:** conceptual reframing, installation evolution, dialogue rules, synchronised simulation and exhibition experience.

Prioritise candidates that show:

- final two-computer enclosure clearly;
- machine vision → machine perspective shift;
- conference-room / talking-object idea simplified into the final installation;
- opposing identities and alternating interface states;
- headphones and audience access;
- exhibition scale;
- one dashcam image only when it clarifies the turning point.

Cut or hold:

- generic AI imagery;
- frames implying a live AI exchange when the exhibited version was pre-recorded;
- arbitrary chat screenshots;
- duplicate installation angles;
- images implying identities emerged without prior role-setting.

**Round 02 shortlist target:** 9–12 candidates  
**Final release target:** 7–9 images

### 6.3 Audi Integrated Brand Communication

**Required proof:** professional scale, integrated communication, production coordination and contribution across three subcases.

Prioritise candidates that show:

- one premium portfolio opener;
- one outcome and one scale / production image for each subcase;
- one publication-safe planning artefact proving narrative or coordination contribution;
- visual balance across Audi Channel, A8L Horch and Audi × Ryuichi Sakamoto.

Cut or hold:

- generic car beauty shots;
- repetitive celebrity screenshots;
- unverified metrics;
- confidential supplier or client documents;
- images implying sole authorship;
- uncredited third-party photography.

**Round 02 shortlist target:** 10–14 candidates  
**Final release target:** 7–10 images

### 6.4 Deear Magazine

**Required proof:** editorial pacing, publication structure, typography hierarchy and contribution to a context for personal expression.

Prioritise candidates that show:

- real cover or strongest opening spread;
- consecutive spreads rather than isolated favourites;
- hierarchy and reading rhythm;
- complete publication object;
- two distinct spread families;
- one marked proof or process image when it shows an editorial decision.

Cut or hold:

- mock-ups stronger than the real historical result;
- visually similar spreads;
- contributor material without permission;
- images that overstate art direction or total publication ownership;
- layouts selected only for surface aesthetics.

**Round 02 shortlist target:** 8–11 candidates  
**Final release target:** 6–8 images

### 6.5 Madam, in Eden, I'm Adam

**Required proof:** storyboard, symbolic sequence, shoot execution, hand performance and post-production consistency.

Prioritise candidates that show:

- one signature image;
- the intended beginning, development and cyclical closure;
- full edited sequence in correct order;
- storyboard paired with final frame;
- one gesture or symbol detail only when it clarifies the narrative.

Cut or hold:

- reordered sequence for visual variety;
- near-identical colour grades;
- generic fashion mock-ups;
- behind-the-scenes material with unclear credits;
- claims of sole photography or creative direction not supported by evidence.

**Round 02 shortlist target:** 7–9 candidates  
**Final release target:** 5–7 images

## 7. Portfolio-wide coherence pass

After project-level scoring, review all KEEP and MAYBE images together.

Check:

1. Is there one dominant hero per project?
2. Are too many images using the same composition or scale?
3. Does every image add new information?
4. Are research, system, outcome and experience balanced across the full portfolio?
5. Does the visual rhythm support the archive rather than create five disconnected mini-portfolios?
6. Are image treatments historically respectful rather than artificially homogenised?
7. Are real outcomes consistently prioritised over decorative enhancement?

## 8. Contact-sheet protocol

Create one contact sheet per project using this order:

1. KEEP
2. MAYBE
3. HOLD
4. CUT

Each thumbnail must display:

- temporary filename;
- role code;
- score;
- decision;
- one-line reason.

Recommended contact-sheet filename:

`[PROJECT]_ROUND_02_CONTACT_SHEET.pdf`

## 9. Rename protocol

Rename candidates only after the initial cut.

Format:

`[PROJECT]_[ROLE]_[NUMBER]_[DECISION].[ext]`

Examples:

- `TYRANNO_S_01_KEEP.jpg`
- `INFINITE_O_02_MAYBE.png`
- `AUDI_D_01_HOLD.pdf`
- `DEEAR_O_03_KEEP.tif`
- `MADAM_X_01_KEEP.jpg`

Do not renumber final sequence order yet. Round 02 numbering identifies candidates, not page order.

## 10. Round 02 completion criteria

Round 02 is complete when:

- every available candidate has a role code and decision;
- duplicates are removed;
- all HOLD reasons are explicit;
- every essential Round 01 role is either filled or marked MISSING;
- each project has a compact shortlist within the target range;
- all shortlisted images can be reviewed together as contact sheets;
- no shortlisted image relies on false project status or overstated authorship.
